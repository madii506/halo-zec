06

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. halo pays pump.fun's launch cost from its own wallet. You don't send SOL, you don't connect a wallet and you don't sign anything.

Who gets the fees?

The holders. Each coin is created with a 1% fee that pump.fun pays to the people holding it, in ZEC. There is no creator wallet taking a cut.

Is ZEC on Solana real Zcash?

It's ZEC carried over to Solana as a token. To hold it as shielded Zcash, use Shield: it sends it to your own Zcash address through NEAR Intents.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Memecoins are risky and most go to zero. Nothing here is financial advice.
